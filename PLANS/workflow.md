# worflow saver

Workflow saver  is an extension of the omarchy workspaces saver. It saves and demonstrates
a user workflow and the necessary configs. A restore button allow to restore the previous workflow.t

As a demonstrator its saves a picture of all non empty workspaces, including the scratchpaed workspace the `.config/` hierarchy
of the apps and commands that runs under the omarchy shell
It is up to the user to create a video of his workflow.

following format.
